# Survey on Video and Multimodal Generation

## TL;DR
- Core technologies and architectures like diffusion models and hierarchical attention have led to significant advancements in video generation.
- Current training paradigms include multimodal warmup and Co-Reinforcement Learning, optimizing interactions between modalities.
- Benchmarks like UniVBench and MSVBench address significant evaluation gaps in the multimodal domain, leading to improved understanding and generation capabilities.
- Emerging tools exemplify the shift towards intricate and synchronized production of videos across various domains.

## Background
Video and multimodal generation are rapidly evolving fields that merge visual data with audio and textual information. The growth in consumer demand for interactive media, alongside advancements in computation power, has driven significant research in these areas. Foundational works have outlined key methodologies, with recent advancements focusing on integrating diverse data types for enhanced creative outputs.

## Core Technologies and Architectures
Various innovative architectures have emerged, including diffusion models which facilitate high-fidelity video generation. These technologies emphasize improving both computational efficiency and quality of output, reaching resolutions as high as 4K. Additionally, techniques like semi-autoregressive prediction and hierarchical attention mechanisms are being employed to create more immersive and contextually relevant video outputs. [1][2]

## Training and Optimization Paradigms
Current paradigms for training multimodal generation models focus on methods such as multimodal warmup and feature pre-scaling. Frameworks like Co-Reinforcement Learning are making strides in optimizing multimodal interactions, significantly enhancing model performance in generating coherent and context-rich responses across visual and textual modalities. [2][3]

## Empirical Benchmarks and Applications
Empirical benchmarks have become integral in assessing the effectiveness of video generation models. The introduction of metrics like UniVBench and MSVBench aims to cover evaluation gaps in multimodal reasoning. Emerging architectures, such as CtrlVDiff, are pivotal in unifying capabilities of understanding and generating video, thereby serving a broad range of applications, from entertainment to education. [3][4]

## Emerging Frontiers
Innovative trends point towards enhanced synchronization in video generation, with tools like Kandinsky pushing the envelope on creative applications. These advancements emphasize unifying various data modalities to produce high-quality outputs efficiently, thereby offering promising pathways in both creative and industrial applications. [5][6]

## Trends and Open Problems
Over the last two years, the landscape of video and multimodal generation has seen rapid advancements, yet several unresolved questions remain. These include understanding the long-term impacts of newly developed architectures on creative industries and addressing the need for standardized benchmarks that can universally assess model performance. [3][4]
